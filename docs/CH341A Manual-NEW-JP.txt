24 Chip: BIOS Rewrite Guide
Required items:

CH341A ROM Writer
SOP8 Clip compatible with EEPROM 93CXX / 25CXX / 24CXX
Another PC
Required software (available in the downloaded folder):
Programmer USB Driver
CH341A Programmer Software

Product Description
CH341A ROM Writer
SOP8 Clip
Using this clip, you can directly connect to the chip without removing it. This guide uses the 24 series chip as an example (the 25 series explanation is at the end).
Disconnect the motherboard from the PC and power off to rewrite the BIOS ROM on the motherboard.
Connect the CH341A ROM Writer to the BIOS chip using the SOP8 clip.

Insert the jumper cap into pins 1 and 2 to enable programmer mode.
Align the red line with pin 1 of the chip.
Rotate the arm (which looks like an antenna) 90° upwards, insert the pin into the programmer, then return the arm to lock the pin in the programmer.


Install the driver -- XP, Vista, 7, 8, 8.1, 10 - 32bit 64bit
A. Click "CH341SER" in the downloaded folder.
B. Click [安装] (Install) in Chinese, and close the window when done.
C. Check if the installation was successful.
Insert the writer into the PC and open the Device Manager to check for the port. If the port is present, the USB driver was successfully installed.

2) Install CH341A Software
After installing the driver, insert the CH341A ROM Writer into the computer's USB port, open the "Programmer software" folder, and click "CH341A".
When "Device state: Connected" appears in the bottom right, you are ready to program.
3) Start the Rewrite


Click "Chip Search" to find the chip in use. This guide uses the AT24C32 chip as an example.


Click "Read".


Click "Open" to open the edited .hex file.


After loading the file, click "Auto". Wait for the message indicating a successful update.


USB TTL Serial Adapter Setup


Install the USB TTL driver -- XP, Vista, 7, 8, 8.1, 10 - 32bit 64bit
Install another driver for USB TTL serial mode. Open the "Driver" folder in the downloaded folder and click "CH341SER 2014INF".
Click [Install] and close the window when done.


Change the jumper cap
Insert the jumper cap into pins 2 and 3 to enable TTL serial mode.


Insert into the computer's USB port
After installing the TTL driver, insert the CH341A ROM Writer into the computer's USB port, and you are ready to program.

Disconnect the motherboard from the PC and power off to rewrite the BIOS ROM on the motherboard.
Refer to the diagram for connecting the ROM writer and clip:
Align the red line with pin 1 of the chip. Pin 1 has a black dot. Refer to the diagram.


Install the driver -- XP, Vista, 7, 8, 8.1, 10 - 32bit 64bit
A. Click "CH341SER" in the downloaded folder.
B. Click [安装] (Install) in Chinese, and close the window when done.
C. Check if the installation was successful.
Insert the writer into the PC and open the Device Manager to check for the port. If the port is present, the USB driver was successfully installed.


Install CH341A Software
After installing the driver, insert the CH341A ROM Writer into the computer's USB port, open the "Programmer software" folder, and click "CH341A".
When "Device state: Connected" appears in the bottom right, you are ready to program.


Click "Chip Search" to find the chip in use. This guide uses the GD25Q16 chip as an example. Enter GD25Q16, select it, and click "Select".
Click "Detect" to detect the chip. If detected, the chip information will be displayed on the left side of the software. If the connection is poor or the chip is incompatible, the information will show $FF.
Start the Rewrite

(Backup the original data of the chip) Click "Read". The progress bar will appear on the left side.
When the progress bar reaches 100%, click "Save" to save the data.
After saving, click "Erase". Click "确认" (Confirm) when the message appears.
